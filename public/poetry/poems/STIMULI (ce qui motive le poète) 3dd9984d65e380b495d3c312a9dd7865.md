# STIMULI (ce qui motive le poète)

Date: December 26, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Philosophical
Theme: Conflict & Choice

The moment,

The mission,

The miser; entwined.

Etching the canvas,

Scribing neurons for pleasure.

The sire,

The sermon,

The speech; enshrined.

Burning the fields with hot coals from the temple.

*This cult of foul reason,*

As if red, white July,

Add a blue, for the hues of harsh herald rhymes.

These tempers,

Those furies,

Those silenced; divine.

Place portraits in minds of the paupers, whom write.
