# Orphan

Date: July 18, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

I write for that child seldom seen or heard
Whom yearns for the comfort of anger or seething,
Instead given apathy no love and no feeling,
Heart petrifies, scared of the future: a cage

The orphan boy, leech whom steals small delights,
How bad must he be to be thieving gangrene gutters,
His insides twisted, turgid,
His mind sunless and cold,
Visage did turn to stone no way to know if teeth still grow.

The orphan boy, flirting in dreams with death and dark,
Small pleasures till in waking he does realise life’s still hard,
And cruel, and violent, for others whose light’s dim,
The orphan boy feels nothing, as no one does feel for him
