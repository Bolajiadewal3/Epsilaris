# Natural Order

Date: October 1, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Nature-focused
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

‘Tis the natural order,

To praise fauna in the corners we call crevices,
Out of sight and mind for those whistle,
As they pass by and see no green,
Colour-blind to worlds beneath their feet so full of folly;
Some heathens to my world.

For it’s the natural order,

To see flora when in saunas as,
The heat does melt the mind and make one dream of milk-blue skies;
And just below see flowers fight for light through plight and petty squabbles:
Parading their better sides for minds that fly and move their seed.

For it is my natural order,

To see this speck of dust as is and appreciate it evermore,
One smaller cog, in this space; this universe is law,
Yet still I see the speck – still call it Earth – love it for its flaws,
One sees the green, does smile and weave tapestries: odes, in awe.
