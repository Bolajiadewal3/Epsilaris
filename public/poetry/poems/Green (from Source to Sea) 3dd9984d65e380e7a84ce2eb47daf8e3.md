# Green (from Source to Sea)

Date: July 4, 2025
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Conflict & Choice

It was simple,

Or at least, supposed to be,

*Walk along the path*,

Seek a life to lead.

How distant and foolish,

Those words seem now,

Stuck ‘tween two walls,

Fear etched on the brow.

The day started as usual,

Eyes flickered back to life,

Routine done, and ready,

Lost salience ‘fore the hike.

As I stare into the darkness,

The path loses its appeal,

*Left too late,* they said,

*Left behind,* I think, still.

Descended the hill we did,

The heavens spilled water in sync,

Too step to reclimb,

Too slippery,
