# Sleeper

Date: July 28, 2025
Movement: Surrealism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Dreams & the Uncanny

A whisper heard but twice in the year,
The modern man did forget his form,
For he was a maker, myth-maker: thrall,
He was a saviour for paupers whom called.

The dark of the night or the brow of the morrow,
The grimace of dawn upon nocturnal eyes,
The sleepless did see he did save them from slumber,
Where dreams scared the folk, in their caves, alone.

Yet now so forsaken,
The mode but an art,
No longer a science,
Some deem it a farce.

The preachers lost summers,
No muse for the hard,
His era is dead,
Thus his death feels fleeting.
