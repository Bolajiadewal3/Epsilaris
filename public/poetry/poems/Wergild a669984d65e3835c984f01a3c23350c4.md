# Wergild

Date: March 10, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Philosophical
Theme: Conflict & Choice

The silence of salience lingered in slumber,
Replaced upon wake by the virile sense,
Of violence, vitriol, valiance through plunder,
Minds of the makers, met with and wrenched.

Whereforth be payment,
Wherein coin meant,
Whose sin bade the vraiment,
Own up or be spent.

Proclaimed by the miser,
Whomst slew babe in bed,
Crown cradles none wiser,
liege lay: lost of head.

Confessions of silence,
Vows burned through the eyes,
Not one dared compliance,
To pawn for cleaved lives.
