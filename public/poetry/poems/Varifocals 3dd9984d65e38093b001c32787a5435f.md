# Varifocals

Date: September 26, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Transformation & Growth

If not for derelict days,
The mind of the miser would not seek higher pleasures.

For the solace breeds a want,
This, visceral temptation,
The writer needs pain – to elevate prose.

So one shifts the lens: dark blood-tints do no favours,
Must relearn the ways – to see the world.

The muse must be morphed,
From mere spectre to haven,
The form of the fierce must change to the flawed.

Find a home in one’s idols,
Or be destitute folly,
For said space will bring peace for the pleasure of all.

As the scribbles birth sonnets,
One’s prose fights the feud,
So on dull and dark days,
Now the poet sees hues.
