# Fauna

Date: June 1, 2026
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Romantic
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Love & Desire

Sweetness in the air,
That strikes the palate fiercely,
Fills my floor with feelings of flirtation and fauna.

The breath of the birds: alive,
One hears love conveyed in quartets,
The life unlike one’s own is rife with pleasure, pain and place.

Even darkness hates these hills,
It feels guilty for disturbing,
The field of fae and spirit souls whom hover higher hearths.

The end of life is worth it:
Here,
Immortal be the mode.

This scene, serene and flirting with my essence: it be home.
