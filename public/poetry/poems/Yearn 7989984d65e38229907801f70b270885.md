# Yearn

Date: February 15, 2024
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Romantic
Theme: Love & Desire

Love is learned,
Yet I yearn for it.

To crave another’s soul without carnal intent,
Is foreign to me.

To hurt from another heart,
Is figment for me.

To fight for a futile farce,
Is brave to see.

To whomever watches the watchers,
Or whistles through winds,
To the traitor of tyranny,
Shah of skies that sing
Satiate this soul and strike one with serendipity.

Gift one a love of lifetimes left lost,
Even if for a day,
Let the knowledge bring forth pain,
Yet for that day paint pleasure,
And thereafter, melancholy.
