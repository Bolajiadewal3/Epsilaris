# Twist

Date: September 26, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Philosophical
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

To say that this world is derelict – is to say that it is devoid of meaning,
Yet to say it is devoid – is to say that it is destitute in value.

Upon which word should one’s interpretation lay,
Whose view holds the whole portrait,
When then should one select the term and apply it unequivocably to describe the dilapidation of this seldom sought world.

If it was better, good even, surely others would come,
Flock to join us in jovial jubilance and justify their emigration,

Yet,
Nothing,
To say the universe is silent, - would be to state it holds no sound,
Yet to say it is of no sound is to say it holds no celestial bodies.

The choice of word, matters.
