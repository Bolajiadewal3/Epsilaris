# Chaser

Date: October 1, 2025
Movement: Surrealism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Dreams & the Uncanny

Heard fragments, figments or prose in between,
Of a figure divine: a sanctioned being,
That holds sway on the dreary,
Shocks them to life.

So went hunting the solace,
Of slumber and time.

Did fail one could say,
For my god seems far stronger,
Or maybe it’s I with no will to stay free,
Eyes open forced shut by my god of forced dreams,
Eyes open lacked luck to see creatures from fables.

I guess I lack danger,
Or power,
Or both.

Might swerve so lethargic,
To force its hand,
How sweet must it be,
To glimpse gods in their vibrance.

Still chasing its knees,
Still waiting with license.
