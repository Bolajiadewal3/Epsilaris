# A Meeting

Date: November 5, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

One often yearns for the past,
Before conception of the sole,
To bask in the brilliance of one’s progenitors.

To know the pair as peer,
To know family as if fellow,
To enjoy their unique labours as if spectator in place of prize.

One chance to meld the minute,
To sculpt the second and soar,
Reaching heights unseen by kin and creeping ever closer.

To the day when deeds redeem,
When the thoughts be more than mental,
Yearning for a time when meeting makers is no fiction.

The figment from forefathers,
Doth darken days of mine,
The bloodline yearns for congruence.

Let me meet my mind.
