# Ten

Date: June 1, 2026
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Nature-focused
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

From abode to the spire, antecedent mode.
Now here, admiring the world at one’s feet.
The seconds turned years while one’s breaths stretch for decades,
The silence once suffered; now healing the muse.

The scent,
Serene,
As if scraped from sweating angels,
Contrasting below: newt-Nephilim nether.

The rapture,
Alive,
The heavens hath opened,
Up here ‘bove the clouds: my soul is alone.
