# University

Date: September 15, 2026
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Nature-focused
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

Arrival brings reason, remorse or regret,
At the end of the platform lays pleasure and pleasing,
Which way to the future, unknown by the worthy,
Used will and their wisdom to reach this new meaning.

Could wade in the water, or dive to the depths,
Drowning in liquors, or floating in feeling,
The wisdom was dealt, by beings outside reason,
The mission is keeping the brain through the wreck.

The pier is safe,
The blue holds threats,
First month on the sea is the trial for the wretched.

The ocean is crazy,
Takes lives from one’s peerage,
And holds them hostage, so far from the home.

The depths once deadly, soon speak to the soul,
Three years and the victim, stands tall in the moat.

Yet now on the platform, one stares through the cold,
Sees specks of starlight reflected and rogue.

Through blue stars are bleeding,
The mirror holds modes,
Distorted and altered, the water is weaving.

The dock blocks the dealings,
Once landed: you’re home,
Your actions in water are left at the coast,
You step on the pier and,
Reflect on the soul,
Twisted and turned: not what left, the home.
