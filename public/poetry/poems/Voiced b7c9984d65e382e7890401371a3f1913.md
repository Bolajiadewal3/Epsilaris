# Voiced

Date: November 13, 2025
Movement: Spoken Word
Sub-Theme One: Social & Political
Theme: Conflict & Choice

The voices fight for a place on the pedestal,
Steering the minds of the folk in the room,
Fake praise for the loudest,
Faint praise for the best,
Opinions are barrelled and fired at chest.

This section is silent,
My space is a zone,
Of peace: for I prosper ‘mongst dismal fiends.

This section once violent,
Now peaceful, serene,
The liege: for I holster rebuttals for free.

When the battle’s near done,
And their eyes start to dare,
All heads turn to I,
The prince of the peace.

One recalls the events,
The actions,
The words,
Response will decide,
Who is victor,
Who’s first.
