# New

Date: September 29, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Romantic
Theme: Love & Desire

Unknown and unnerving,
The beat flutters,
Harmonies ruined by a dame,
Today.

Glimpse futures of passions,
Pains and desires,
The present needs work,
For those futures to stay.

Serene, in bliss,
A beauty, an angel,
The kinfolk of cherubs,
Must smell sweet to the bone.

Pristine, remiss,
Adoring her fables,
She writes with a mode,
That entices this tone.

Hence this auteur write rambles,
The thoughts turned to prose,
Figure catches the eyes,
Leave the writer in throes.

Still one skipped a beat,
Nervous, never, yet she,
Brings desire and themes,
Of soul ties to this
