# Mind

Date: May 31, 2026
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

This itch on my mind is unnerving. Or maybe it’s the mode alone, striving for greatness yet failing with grace; dodging all failure by inviting it all. Or maybe one’s tired, of fighting, of popping pain pustules.

The journey was winding, is winding: swerving. The journey holds terror, this journey is cruel.

Unlike my own makers, my mind is alone – no love to begrudge with this burden of glory. Unlike my life taker, I’m free from it all: the wound killing kin, yet releasing my shackles.
