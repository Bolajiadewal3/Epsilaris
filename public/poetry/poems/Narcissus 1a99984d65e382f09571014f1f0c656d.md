# Narcissus

Date: September 26, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

The soul holds sway over cosmic misfortunes,
Shift the lens and view failings as lessons to loathe.

The darkness delays that onslaught of violence,
Membranes see the light and assault the brains core.

For I spent half a life, in my mind, with my vices,
Saw cosmic horrors painted with hues from my moat.

May have mixed the proportions, mistaken my throne,
King of minutiae deemed himself king of all zones.
