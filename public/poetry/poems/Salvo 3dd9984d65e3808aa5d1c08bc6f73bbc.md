# Salvo

Date: June 1, 2026
Movement: Ecopoetry
Sub-Theme One: Nature-focused
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

Restless,
The madness infects all the folk,
The air turning turgid and crude in its mode.

Been a while in the making,
They’re winning with woe,
Have no answer for dealing with mechanised crows.

Through the grey one sees green,
Tiny sprout in the spray,
Beautiful: near-fiction for future folk fables.

Took the green from the grey,
And buried it deep,
To be born anew,
In my garden:
My keep.
