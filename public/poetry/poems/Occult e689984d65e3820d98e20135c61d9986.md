# Occult

Date: February 15, 2024
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Identity & Isolation

Where-forth lays reason, pain and peace.
Wherein lies patience, wisdom or fame.
Triads of life samsaric in existence,
Whereby lays salvation,
Where pleasure,
Why unearthed and uncoiled still.
By who’s decree,
And who’s making,
By whom does fortune favour,
By who did one evolve.
What strings were played to place one present,
What harmony synthesised,
What resonance relayed.
From whence did one appear,
And to who soon join.
