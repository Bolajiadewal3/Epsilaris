# Lone

Date: February 14, 2024
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Love & Desire

I’ve learned to love alone,
Myself and my thoughts,
Manifesting lives of wisdom and wonder,
In a head so small.

I’ve learned to love the lone,
Space which I call mine,
Yet yearn for company,
Awkward silence and laughter.
