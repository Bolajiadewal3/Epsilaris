# Two

Date: November 13, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Grief & Loss

Half a decade gone,
A single face seen,
How flawed were my eyes that perceived no fiend.

Laughter and languish,
The lie taints the scene,
How foul be your flowers that taunt me in dreams.

Did happen before,
Thought friend was the core,
How selfish for wasting prime years on your team.

Two lessons enforced,
Two faces: one dawn,
Other dusk, rakish heathen, in mind: you’re deceased.
