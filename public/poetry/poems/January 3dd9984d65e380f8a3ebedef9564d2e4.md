# January

Date: September 15, 2025
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Transformation & Growth

Born anew,
Or torn asunder,
Formations of fragments from previous years,
The pain still solid,
The solvent sears,
The mind still a mire of crystal tears.

Born anew,
Or reborn as thunder,
Small difference ‘tween prose: one signals renewal,
This giant is weeping,
Diamond ducts loose rain,
Golden streaks on the face of this lord in lieu.

Born anew,
In lieu of the hunger,
Wealth and fame couldn’t fill this moat,
The cravings are crazed,
The silence does wonders,
As new as a newt: one must re-earn your vote.
