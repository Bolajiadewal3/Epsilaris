# Integrals

Date: September 26, 2025
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme One: Experimental
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Grief & Loss

The edge, the limit of the sum of one’s devices,
Used in tandem to shock the soul.

The silent onus of the destitute poet,
Lending credence to claims of uncaptured woes.

Some slip through the cracks,
Others flee through the action,
Meaning missed and words whispered as opposed to loud whisks.

Some slide through the vault,
Whence one stored their pain-pleasures,
Now lost to the binary faults that make up the brain.

The edge, the path on which thoughts don’t return,
Used as the point on which memories blur.

The silent soldier, whom writes for the world,
Lends credence to notions that prose, does end.
