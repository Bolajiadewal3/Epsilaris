# Heartbreak

Date: October 1, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Grief & Loss

How does one start again at fifty,

How does one find paths unsoiled.

Had love and lust intertwined, or so,

I thought, thought wrong, was wronged, now cold.

How does one find a job for those,

Less able in body yet sharp in soul,

Or soon to be sharp, right now its shattered;

Smelt new satin sheets due to linen’s new master,

Mean. heard from the cloth, the entire façade,

Through the scented airs one heard grinning and laughter,

Champagne or the wine, both did swim in the sheets.

The old ones, forgotten, left lying in heat.

How does one start again at fifty,

Heard cries from faux hearts ‘tween the two whom belayed,

Betrayal and the shock that my mind worked in phase.

How simple a scene,

*Why new satin sheets?*

Lie crumbled so quickly I deemed it unseen.

How does one start again at fifty,

The edge of one’s heart now be facing the fire,

Explaining to peers why the ring has no sire,

Explaining to youth that one’s life’s never safe.
