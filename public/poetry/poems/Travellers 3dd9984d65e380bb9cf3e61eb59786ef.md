# Travellers

Date: December 4, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Conflict & Choice

Dark and dangerous waters waded,
To reach lands unseen by own,
That world is new,
That world is wondrous,
Lies told to appease: half-truths in mode.

Yet life was lived by my forebears fully,
Smiles and tears from the best of my folk,
My idols feared all the frolic follies,
The strangers who stare and whisper in boasts.

Decisions were made,
In darkness and danger,
To spawn a second,
Prince of the prose.

In lands unholy,
This sire arose,
This wergild for ancestors stolen from home.

Decisions were made,
Post-nascent for more,
To make my path:
So simple and grown.

Myself - the scholar,
Ill replicate roles,
Move to lands unseemly,
And birth finer souls.
