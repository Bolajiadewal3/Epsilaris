# Doomed One

Date: October 7, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Transformation & Growth

It burns,
So bright one thought heavens hugged the frame,
Embracing folk and freeing souls the burn was bright and named,

The blaze,
Crafted by carnal king liars,
Whom lured their champion back,
Through Shebas and gold.

Forsaken one,
Heart held high once heaved from the chest,
Blistered and battered near frayed to the bone,
The golden one fell to the flames entire.

How deep was the moat,
Surrounding his ire,
The king couldn’t keep such a prince in his home.

The blessed one,
Beat odds and partied in glory,
The king grew impatient,
Ones myth grew through fire.

The holy one,
Gods favourite fixing the Sun,
Ascended in form after suffering
