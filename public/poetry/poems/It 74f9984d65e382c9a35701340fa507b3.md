# It

Date: July 28, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Conflict & Choice

Be not afraid despite destined disaster,
For I am the prison of your pained self sleep.

This action of yours forced a heat for your furnace,
No fuel as myth-makers do not need such sway.

This being chooses its battles,
Once did save one from lannes,
Thus again kill the slumber,
You’re in lane, almost dead.
