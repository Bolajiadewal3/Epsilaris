# Race

Date: August 31, 2025
Movement: Spoken Word
Sub-Theme One: Social & Political
Theme: Identity & Isolation

I see the stares
In them lie ancestors
Guilty or otherwise

I see the stares
In them is hatred
Eyes twitching to their temper

I see the stares and the smiles lack sync
Can’t help themselves but to feign their affection
I see the stares and I think, how dim
Must the light inside be upon their reflection

Soul searching, found nothing
Brain biopsies fail
No minds of their own just a vitriol spread

Half measures and musings
Half hearted attempts
To appease my people while stares wish us dead
