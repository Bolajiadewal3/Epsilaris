# Voice of a ‘Miser’

Date: December 4, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Grief & Loss

I was fired today.

Ten years of hard labour turned turgid and tame,

As if used as a reason to render mine frayed,

Or useless, denying the context in phase.

So let me explain.

Lost my way for a day,

Had enough of the musings ‘bout rice or the rain,

*Does it rain where you’re from*,

Disrespectful dark notion,

Lost my life for responding as ancestors would.

I don’t feel any pain,

But how quick pleasure shifted,

I believed that my place was on pedestals of saints.

Instead I was flirting,

With ruin every day,

Come to find that my role fit the quota: for fame.

I was fired today.

Mean to face anyway,

I was fired the moment I signed to this state,

My fate in the fire, now this pyre lacks praise.

*Keep your head up,*

Unruly, fake smiles to the face,

I was fired today, never safe in this maze.
