# Teetotal

Date: December 4, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Grief & Loss

The truth, when told, can cut through hearts so true,
As if meaning folds from the weight of the words: crushed.
The silence so seemly, now prayed for and loved,
The truth scarred the room with an air of demeaning.

Describe the teller, the best of the folk,
Yet sat with a stare of misfortunate reason,
The demon took hold, from the bottle its feelings,
Seeped through the membranes, neurons were sold.

Tomorrow, regret, one word for it all,
Today, the pain takes a hold of the crowd,
That truth pierced the ceiling, inhibitions and woe,
Her tongue formed the motions, the sword to their souls.
