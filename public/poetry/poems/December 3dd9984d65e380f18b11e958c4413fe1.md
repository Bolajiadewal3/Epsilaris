# December

Date: September 15, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

Decembrists held session and deemed their cause worthy,
Last chance to instil in the masses: a mode,
Of violent resistance and silent obsession,
With lessons on seeing, the self and the soul.

Take lessons from learnings,
The past feeds the post,
Post-childhood, now grown one must yield to the yearning,
To know one’s self, one’s essence, one’s whole,
To choose one’s actions, not slaves one’s ghosts.

Take session and study,
That Russian revolt,
While ideals stem from peace – violence vilifies votes,
Hold the facts to the frow,
Impart knowledge and teachings,
From their deaths bore a code: last month signals revealing.
