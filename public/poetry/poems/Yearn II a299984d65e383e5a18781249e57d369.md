# Yearn II

Date: September 1, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Romantic
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Love & Desire

Does this feeling ever seize,
Does this heart truly believe,
That it will find that hope again,
Where the world is right and right makes sense,
Where the beauty in brilliance lays naked: stares,
Back at my eyes, in love, intent.

Bear with my passion,
Prose from the soul,
That body was holy; mind: dark and dull.

That brilliance in beauty held sway on my mould,
Now eyes wide open, see demons in dolls.

Betrayed by your mission,
Aghast at your tone,
You slighted my heart, left scars on my bones.
