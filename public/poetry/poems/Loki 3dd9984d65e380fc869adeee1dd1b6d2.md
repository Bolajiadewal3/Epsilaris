# Loki

Date: December 16, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Conflict & Choice

The façade has been set,
Multifaceted,
Fiendish,
The lie does now lie on the sermons of woe.

For melded in meaning,
Be truths yet untold,
To hide them is lying,
Equated in mode.

Yet the lie do be needed,
Their neurons implode,
When facts fall from fragments of fear and hit folds.

The lie do be stealing,
The hurt from their souls,
They view this as evil,
One sees it as bold.
