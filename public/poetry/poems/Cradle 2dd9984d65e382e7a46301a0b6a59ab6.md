# Cradle

Date: September 10, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Time & Memory

The moment.

A sought and seldom seen scene of serenity.

The purest picture pondered upon by the mind of the prince on their pyre.

The clearest crease of clarity on a canvas covered in crud.

The moment.

Too harsh for unworthy,
Salivated on by the best,
Reaching their twilight still waiting for the sight.

The moment.

Tremendous occasion,
Too talented a day to pass by,
Its date set in stone by starry-eyed angels.

The moment.
