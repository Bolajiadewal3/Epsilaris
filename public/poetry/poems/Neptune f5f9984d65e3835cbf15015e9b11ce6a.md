# Neptune

Date: November 13, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Philosophical
Theme: Conflict & Choice

The soul feels the pressure and responds with a jest,
For the mind be the court where the languish is kept,
In dark corners,
Hidden,
Festered and feasting,
On cries from the youth,
And lies from the heathens.

Whose choice was it first?

To trade coin for credence,
Who swapped out their hand full of grain for the gold,
Did doom us entire,
Did gift us with prose,
Treaties on torture to claim wealth from woe.

Whose choice is it now?

To maintain these dark dealings,
Invading the psyche with the diamonds one chose,
To give too much meaning,
To raise for a host.

The thief feels the pressure,
Blames himself,
Blames the globe.
