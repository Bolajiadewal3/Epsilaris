# Brit

Date: December 15, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Grief & Loss

The nuance lost,
The curtains closed,
The horror of words unsaid by my hosts.

Hear silence in laughter,
When speaking in prose,
Unlike what was learned when elders weren’t grown.

Does one place the blame,
At the feet of these fulcrums,
Whose wisdom has birthed: a prince for the age.

Still hurting, disabled,
Disarmed in the ways,
Of father’s mum’s tongue: I’m removed from my saints.
