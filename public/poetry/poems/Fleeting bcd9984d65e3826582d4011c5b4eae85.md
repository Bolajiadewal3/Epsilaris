# Fleeting

Date: September 18, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Transformation & Growth

That flicker,
Could it be?
One’s path to salvation.

That crumb,
Seldom comes,
One’s way to the world.

That glint,
In the stardust,
Made maker from paste.

That crack,
In the crevice,
Bore wonders and fame.

That flicker,
Could it be?
One’s route to resolve.

That crumb,
Seldom seen,
Holds weight in its woes.

That glint,
When lost,
Births derision and silence.

That crack,
In the crease,
It leaves:
Finds another.
