# Siren

Date: February 11, 2024
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Romantic
Theme: Love & Desire

What fleeting feeling one pursues,
Chasing pain of pleasure for fear of failure,
Whispering words of witch wisdom,
Useful only for cauldrons of nonsense and spew.

Describe your faculties and hear back your response,
Think of self-make and fear the babe,
If one’s progeny mirrored you, would love still reside.

Sirens sing of salience,
Yet play with said questions,
Tempting one to run from answers,
And drown in decadence.

Tricksters and spindles,
Fear not what they say,
For it’s been read here before,
And thus you’re prepared.
