# Inquisition

Date: September 29, 2025
Movement: Surrealism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Dreams & the Uncanny

I first glimpsed a miracle when I was young,
Am young still but was younger then,
The perspective differs, my youth as young as now,
In the eyes of a preacher, whose years have fled and cowled.

Back to the miracle, that spectre I did glimpse upon that night,
Laid eyes of mine on sights divine and leapt to learn its tricks.
How the shape has form or vice-versa, I was just a kid,
Sentence structure left at wayside while my brain indulged in trysts.

Flirted with the ghost, a beauty in a life removed from this,
Yet the fascination gripped by soul and left my life a bliss,
A weird, virulent strain of wonder latched itself to membranes since,
Sometimes I glimpse its mode, even now, so far removed from it.
