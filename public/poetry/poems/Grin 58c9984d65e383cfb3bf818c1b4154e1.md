# Grin

Date: September 26, 2025
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme One: Philosophical
Theme: Conflict & Choice

That grin, hated with all my heart,

Precedes code switches and pockets full of promises: scarred.

*I’ll pay it back tomorrow*

Well tomorrow is here,

Fool did fake the feelings,

Tug the strings and disappear in fear.

For he knows the wrath upon him,

Whole country does as he,

Takes blessings from the brethren,

And turns the love to leads:

*Lead me to salvation*

When they lie through lines of white,

*Lead me from temptation,*

Already acted on that vice.

The hypocrite is heathen,

No light-lord would aid their truth,

So distant from the scene yet they hold sway on stars that move,

To distant distant planes in search of paradise and plans,

Astray from corrupt cored hands whom pray yet prey on pauper fans.

Will my land be whole again,

Or did the theft turn it blue.

Will my people learn to lend,

And pay their debts in full.

Will my people learn to love,

Not obey and beat their small.

Will my people learn to make,

A destiny with own tools.
