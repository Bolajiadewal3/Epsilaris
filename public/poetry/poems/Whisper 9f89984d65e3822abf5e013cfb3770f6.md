# Whisper

Date: June 7, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Conflict & Choice

How many whispers, till words wane in meaning,
Melding prose morbid in tone and mode.

Witch welded and slithered, from tongues of misplacing,
Truth tied in a room, locked away from the soul.

The lie,
The lie,
The lie is upon us.

The horror soon made into satire for you,
Yet the lie,
The lie,
The lie do be waiting.

To ruin your small life,
The witch claimed it true.
