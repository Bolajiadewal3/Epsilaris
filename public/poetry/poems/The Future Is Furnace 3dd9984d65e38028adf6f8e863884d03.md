# The Future Is Furnace

Date: December 4, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Philosophical
Theme: Conflict & Choice

Why is fear felt when the future be furnace,
Burning away at the praise and the ire.

This pyre is felt by my hand and one’s hurting,
The burn is a blur, in the moment, this fire.

Does flicker, and flare,
On the night of cold dealings,
The snake round the neck be the fear of the flame,
One’s faith has been tested,
Melded in pain,
Yet coals sear the sky through these eyes of disdain.

Take it,
The leap,
Fall for the test.

If more sorrow is yielded,
Then more training’s been set,
If no change do be gilded,
Then the cliff a false ledge,
If more pleasure is fielded,
The snake leaves the neck.
