# Ode

Date: January 31, 2024
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Time & Memory

If by some miracle,
Proctored by a far away god,
Zeus or Shiva moved me;
Translate my essence through time, not space.

Give young, feeble I a chance,
To meet my progenitors.

When they were twenty-something,
Learning the lessons they instil,
Hiding hardships as I do now,
Smiling through beaten eyes as I do still,
Laughing through cracks of pain unseen.

If I should be so privileged,
To spend a single cycle with them,
Equal in age,
I feel they’d enjoy my company,
And I theirs.
