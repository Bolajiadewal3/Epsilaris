# Dower

Date: February 15, 2024
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Grief & Loss

Which warlock dare speak of wrongs owed,
Or possessions pawned,
Whom wages war with worldly weapons,
Not spirits nor spectres,
To retort on claims so keenly,
As if to hold some semblance of a say.

Yours was lost, too long ago to tear,
Mine is lost, still whispers in ear,
So how can one accost, while I don the black,
Seeks to be dowager,
Is it shame that you lack?
