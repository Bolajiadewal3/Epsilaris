# Paths

Date: February 11, 2024
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Conflict & Choice

Paths lay duplicitous through these eyes,
That perceive futures of self-make and horror,
Futures of success and sorrow.

Paths may diverge and conjoin,
Far beyond view,
But their impressions ripple through time,
And appear as butterflies of bliss.

If one’s future fails,
The past shall prove haven,
Trading faculties for figments,
Truths for lies,
Living inside of one’s mind for eternity.
