# Immortum

Date: March 31, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Conflict & Choice

Where dost thou sleepers slumber?
Or preachers divine fine wisdoms in frames,
Of glory and witch worship,
or husks hideous since name,
Predict denizens of ruin, and riches from rage,

Where forth lies reason, or constituent agents,
Whomst rationales be dead or derelict till waking,
Late learners bled dry to indulge warlock wonder,
Their sequel arrives, teeth stinging the night.
