# Corrections

Date: November 13, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Identity & Isolation

Do my thoughts betray me?
Or was his silence sold?
Whose reasoning led liars, to sell me dreams and hopes.

Or did they too fall flat,
For the tales from wayward does,
Words wilted, corrupted, now falsehoods in my home.

Thought knowledge knew no bounds,
So the pages, torn asunder,
Then told that knowledge frowns,
On those that see it blindly.

Soul cravings for the shroud,
To lift and leave one plunder,
Thought knowledge yielded sounds,
Serene,
Alas the truth is thunder.
