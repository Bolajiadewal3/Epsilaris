# Choose

Date: November 13, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Conflict & Choice

Which should be chosen,
A life of pain or bliss,
For one leaves those ready,
The other in silence.

The best whom boast, don’t suffer,
It dulls the nerves in time,
Those used to pain be clustered,
It feeds and fuels their fame.

Yet they would tell the peaceful,
Don’t change and don’t belay,
Unknowing be the miser,
You’re better with your pain.
