# Sorrow

Date: November 13, 2025
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Grief & Loss

Inner fragments yell harshly at sections of bliss,
Threatening to pierce through their veils as if cysts,
The edges like glass: reflective, amiss,
Skewed by the pain from the past: that mist.

The soul like a sphere,
Of sand-child and burning,
The flames round the hot slab of glass till it’s shaped,
Then the sphere do be broken,
Chipped at,
Frayed,
By blistering nodules of hardship at pace.
