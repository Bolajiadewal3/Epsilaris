# Method

Date: September 25, 2024
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Grief & Loss

Epistles and portraits fulfil in part measures,
Painting the canvas with airs of the soul,
Yet afflictions and maladies creep further inwards,
Denying delight at retrieval and throw,

The mind be the mire, the flesh merely host,
The crag fills with pain, distant darkness and woes,
Thus the etchings in silence lift moods of the folk,
Spilling hues of hot temper, shades of dead does,

Leave the auteur in pain, and profit your pleasures,
For hurt and hate, bear the best of their code,
Leave saints to be martyred, show poets no praise,
For inner violence leaves hunger and hunger, fame.
