# Perspective

Date: August 1, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Nature-focused
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

A speck,
Those glimmers of stardust when rays catch the air,
At the right moment.

To that speck,
We be gargantuan,
As our ‘verse is to us,
This cluster of stalagmite and water,
This home but a mere speck to another.

That other whom looked through their glass,
Found wonderlands and wonders,
