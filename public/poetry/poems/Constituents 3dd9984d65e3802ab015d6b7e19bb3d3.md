# Constituents

Date: September 26, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme Two: Narrative
Theme: Identity & Isolation

It was a normal day,
As like any other,
Wading through imagined deluge on the way.

To join all my peers,
And write ‘spite the jeers,
To pave my own way in the world and stay.

The lectures held sway on the minds of those heathens,
The term here defines those without intellectual fervour.

The masses were lost once her speech hit their sections,
While one was entranced by the view from her world.

Was only a teen,
Young and naïve,
My passions did pour into abject verses.

Was honed by the dean,
My teacher did preach,
My way was with words and my talent was stirring.

The purpose, clear,
Influence tears,
Bring pustules of pleasure to forefronts of seers.

Please masses with prose,
And enlighten the zone,
Cast shadows of saints through nouns from the soul.
