# Prison

Date: September 9, 2025
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

The walls are cold, spewing whispers in hatred,
Trapping the psyche ‘tween four slabs of the stone.

The silence once sacred, now sanctioned and sought:
Employed by the mental as missiles of mischief.

Own mind be aghast,
Own brain misbehaving,
Own paths leaving scars:
Dead doe once was waking.

Now cold to the touch,
Like walls of this prison,
Own psyche be the jailor:
The verdict commissioned.
