# Glass

Date: September 3, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Philosophical
Theme: Conflict & Choice

Pristine, prudent and poorer than most,
Makes for souls enriched with the prose from their ghosts.

Hear whispers and wading,
Torture and sayings,
Hear torment and torrent all wrenched into modes,

Unheard by the masses,
Untouched by the most,
This pauper writes print into parchment for foes.

To read and repent,
Their sermons yield silence,
This scripture opposed: yields cacophonies, choirs.

Rejected be reams,
Of writing on riots,
The pen kisses page to delight of the sirens.

Thrive deep in the depths,
Abyss be their license,
As moody and bleak as the villain of violence.

Young Shelley in dreams,
Does visit this tyrant,
Holds meeting with speak on the state of the soul.

This pursuit be binding,
The end be the most,
Daunting of hosts to be captured in prose.
