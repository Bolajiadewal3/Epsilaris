# Pristine

Date: March 31, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Cosmic
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

A hollowed spectre preserved to the husk,
The scent, serene like the pit of an angel,
Rendered, clean, cured of all cuts,
Its frame is redeemed by its contents and fables.

Stories of stellar, starlight and sterling,
Mixed with stalagmite, crimson, dust,
The words of the worldly, laid plain after yearning,
This body is holy, this body brings lust.
