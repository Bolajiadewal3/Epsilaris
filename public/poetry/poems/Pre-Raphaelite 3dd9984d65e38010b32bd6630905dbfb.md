# Pre-Raphaelite

Date: June 1, 2026
Movement: Surrealism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Dreams & the Uncanny

The remnants of dreamers, I see through their psyche,
As I wade through the sea of green pastures and like,
Elevated myself, in both mind and floor flirting,
Climbed cliffs of chartreuse to glimpse souls of prose saints.

Understanding, gained through these eyes of mine,
Now know how they wrote with such vision: such purpose,
Saw the sky laid out at my feet, saw the world.

Inspired be I, as they were: by the earth.
