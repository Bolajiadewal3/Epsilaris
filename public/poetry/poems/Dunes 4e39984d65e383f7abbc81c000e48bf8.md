# Dunes

Date: February 15, 2024
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Nature-focused
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

Waves of white and dunes of yellow,
Light-scatterings in the minds eye,
Seas of blue uprooted and tilted,
Flipped from where expected,
Rods of mud topped by greens,
Different fir to what I’ve seen,
Different worlds yet here I be,
A second home for a lonely being.
