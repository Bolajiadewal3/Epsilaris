# Flow

Date: September 10, 2025
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Conflict & Choice

That breath upon which a million souls sermons rest,
Praying for your perfection or vilifying your visage,

That breath gives you life, amidst dead hopes and sorrows,
Emotions murdered by the task at hand.

So you inhale and peer,
Into the void of myth-making,
Feeling the burns of pre-destined bliss bathing.

Now exhale; and burst,
Brain built for this tension,
Body’s been poised for the prison or worse.

Mean sentenced by critics,
Whom never lived your life,
Build jails of jarred jargon thrown at useless readers,
Decorum through face, disdain dirts the silence,
Hearts lost if this moment proves more than you’d like.
