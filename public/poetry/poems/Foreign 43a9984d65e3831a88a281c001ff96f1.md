# Foreign

Date: June 18, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

How ajar my own tongue sounds in a land unlike my own,
Yet founded by thine own.

Or perhaps by thine similars, as I claim not my blot of birth, instead my blood and belch.

For I scream in a tongue unlike my mother, yet still am understood.

I scream in modes unlike my father, yet still am comforted.

How they must have felt, I feel now,
Strangers to an strange land,
Heathens upon a Heath unclaimed,
And still I thrive.
