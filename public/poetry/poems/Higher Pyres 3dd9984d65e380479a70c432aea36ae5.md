# Higher Pyres

Date: June 1, 2026
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Grief & Loss

These pyres are higher,
These pyres once false,
Dismissed as misleading non-prescient fables.

These pyres are fierce,
These pyres birth soot,
Decidedly darker when spawned by host’s able.

These pyres bring newts,
These pyres: death-dealers,
Dutifully scraping the earth of its muse.

These pyres burn brightly,
These pyres stay fuelled,
Deep down in those forests,
Deep down out of view.
