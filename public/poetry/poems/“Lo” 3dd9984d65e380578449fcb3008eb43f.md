# “Lo”

Date: June 1, 2026
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

Alone,
Do souls truly suffer in silence?
Or beg for the noise as to blame other’s mouths.

For those ageless wonders,
With wine for blood,
Sit silently waiting for sunlight’s calling.

Are they wrong?
For waiting and wading with whispers,
For holding themselves from the belches and bursts.

Alone,
Do souls truly suffer in silence?
The wisest seem taken: they crave it the most.
