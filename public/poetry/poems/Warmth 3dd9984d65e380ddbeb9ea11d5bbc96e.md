# Warmth

Date: September 15, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Transformation & Growth

That place was a prison,
A pestilent field,
That coloured one’s vision, clouding one’s far-sight.

That place now a home,
A shell broken open,
Took years of mistakes to perceive it whole.

Adolescent angst,
Now dead and cold,
Decays: its flesh now exposed to the crows,
And those crows were feasting,
Left memories and prose,
At the doorstep of I: once kid – now grown.

That place once a prison,
That place now a home,
That place soon a tale of growth and grieving.

The brain now replete,
With life, now whole:
That place once solvent now solves all one’s woes.
