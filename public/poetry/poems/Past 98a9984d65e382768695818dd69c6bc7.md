# Past

Date: October 1, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Time & Memory

It struggles brightly,
Burning yet flawed,
Losing itself to potential, not piety.

It longs for a draw,
Of time-changing sand,
To make different choices and burn oh so lightly.

Too quickly it fluttered,
Flamed and yearned,
Too quick was the frightening: path with the turn.

A blink and its missed,
Scene flashed past the eyes,
Still burning so brightly,  etched in his mind.
