# Fence

Date: May 31, 2026
Sub-Theme One: Social & Political
Theme: Conflict & Choice

The step,
The shout,
Like thunder.

With no lightning, to fear or tear asunder.

The strike,
The jab,
The blunder.

In a past life, would take a soul down under.

Blades see these and laugh: as one laughs at the child.
Whom spits words of war yet has hands yet undefiled.

The crowd,
The cheers,
Abject wonder.

In a past life: tears and bloody plunder.
