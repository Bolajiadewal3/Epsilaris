# Viscera

Date: October 1, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Love & Desire

This body is holy,
This body is framed,
This canvas is bloodied – a positive paste.

So complex is the subject,
Both mental and made,
These neurons do fire – ‘bout neurons innate.

One reflects on reflecting and confuses the codes,
How this brain sees itself is a miracle, crazed.

This body holds pleasure,
Pain and desire,
Ten thousand emotions held up to the fire.

How they fit In this frame,
Understood by none,
Not even a Galen could map these runs.

One deflects on deflection yet is shocked by how stagnant,
Their body remains – echoes mind in its mode.
