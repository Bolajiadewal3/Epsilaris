# Sleepee

Date: August 25, 2025
Movement: Surrealism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Dreams & the Uncanny

I glimpsed it once,
Or him, or her.

That being in its terror did save me at night,
It’s talonic teeth waved and flashed fires,
Some creature beneath all I’d dreamt or believed.

Some saviour of silence,
Or so it proclaimed,
Did leave me silent,
Shocked to the frame.

Did keep me from slumber,
Did slay my own sleep,
Forced my eyes to the road,
As I drove,
At speed.
