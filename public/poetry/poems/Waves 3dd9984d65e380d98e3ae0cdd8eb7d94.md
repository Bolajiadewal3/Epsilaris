# Waves

Date: May 27, 2026
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

Which creature ran amok,
Painting the sea with its glory and glutton,
For it be untamed
Unyielding in way.

Am I therefore, Ulysses or some miser?
For wasting away on this wooden mistake.

Mere metaphors speaking,
The prose is alive,
Used monster to mean one’s owns passions and pyres.

Ten years through this potent,
Painful,
Road.

At war with my mind,
Sailing,
Alone.
