# Blink

Date: September 23, 2024
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Grief & Loss

A blink and it’s missed,
The life of the miser,
A flash of a face,
An imprint of bones,

So quick, so dismissive,
The smile of this prior,
Prince,
whom twisted his faith for the gold,

How dark, how malignant,
His corporeal desires,
Ended the life of a soul,
And his own.
