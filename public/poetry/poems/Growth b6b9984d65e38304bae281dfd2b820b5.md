# Growth

Date: July 30, 2025
Movement: Ecopoetry
Sub-Theme One: Nature-focused
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Transformation & Growth

The young sprout hit the ground running,
Painting the air nearby with prose,
The poet or plant both describing the phenom,
Whom grew into love seldom sought by the folk,

Not toxic or deadly,
But vicious in vibrance,
Orator writing bout previous boasts,
Now reflective and silent,
This tree dodges pyres,
Now grown,
This poet,
Learned love: alone
