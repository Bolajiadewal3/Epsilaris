# The World

Date: November 5, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Conflict & Choice

The world was new,
The world was huge,
The world was torn asunder.

By mythic beasts of prose whom preached,
That life left fast as thunder.

The world is cruel,
The world dulls hues,
The world leaves poets plundered.

By crooks and fiend,
Whom steal the seed,
And doom the worldly wonders.
