# Pyre

Date: September 9, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Social & Political
Theme: Conflict & Choice

How offensive the fire,
Is to the martyr,
Whom wished for a death so quick it was fleeting.

Now burning entire,
Flesh seared to the bone,
This auteur is tired,
This auteurs alone.

For the flames do be higher,
Synonym for the folk,
Whose words spew hot higher air reserved for the most:

Most offensive of liars,
Whomst serenade souls.

They vilify artists,
For painting with prose.
