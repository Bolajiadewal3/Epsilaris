# Mage

Date: February 1, 2024
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Grief & Loss

The dull rod crackled with a flux,
As the grey claimed his peripheral view.

The sharp tinge exalted the air and aether alike, heralding the maiden use of the mahogany slab

Latin or Spanish, phrases unheard whipped the wind like shards of sermon speech

Then:
Cries to a lord blinded,
Cries to a liege centennial,
Pleas to a prince, lethargic,

Stunned whispers and stunted choirs of pain,
As flesh leaves face,
Fascia escapes skin,
Blood frees itself.

Are these the writings of a mage,
Or the account of property,
Joules or spilled haeme,
Wands or wooden pain-makers,
Spells or spelled whips,

Fantasies in the eyes of an infant,
Futile attempts to rationalise the suffering of his silence
The desecration of the dark,
In the dark,
“For the dark”.
