# Beauty

Date: September 1, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Transformation & Growth

The beauty’s in pain,
The pain yields the pleasure,
The process bears burns yet the scars seem sweet.

The paradox persists,
How hurt leads to healing,
This space here is sacred, this mind is serene.

The storm hits the surface,
The brew boils true,
Let it pass and behind it is wealth, golden fruit.

One suffers in silence,
Waits wanting for more,
Soon elated as cravings are answered, by you.
