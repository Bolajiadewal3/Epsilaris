# Cyrus

Date: February 11, 2024
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Grief & Loss

Great,
Greater than,
Dead still.

Legacy of what was willed,
Commissioned and curated,
Killed or claimed,
Great,
Greatest,
Yet dead still.

Damned or deemed,
Derelict or desired,
Woken by the thousand,
Yet slumbering now.

I relieve my pursuits,
Forget my failures,
Pursue only passions,
As greatness will still end with the end.

Greatest,
Yet lying,
Still.
