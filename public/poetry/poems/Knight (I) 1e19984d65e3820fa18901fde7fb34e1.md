# Knight (I)

Date: August 1, 2025
Movement: Surrealism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Dreams & the Uncanny

My night scares yours,
Your darkness follows suit,
Put your demons on the board,
Mine own would kill yours crude.

For my mind is made of mystic horrors,
This pen dumbs down the news,
This meagre mage of crueler sorrows,
Writes prose for you: the muse
