# Accursed

Date: October 4, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Romantic
Theme: Love & Desire

This foul demeanour is but a paste,
Clinging to pores like paint does canvas,
This foul retriever doth serenade dames,
Dorian depths in the mind of this heathen.

How far does it go,
The fault in the feelings,
Unleashing foul beasts whom protest love and growth,

These hideous beasts,
Lain dormant through wearing,
Their mother’s pearl tears all that satiates foul woes.

If the bloodline were crying,
It would paint the ports red,
Soul defects pass porous palisades due to pleasure,

Despite revelations,
Own spawn could be maimed,
In mind by witch-welding,
Five centuries late.
