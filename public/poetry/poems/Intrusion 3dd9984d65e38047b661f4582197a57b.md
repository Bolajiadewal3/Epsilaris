# Intrusion

Date: July 4, 2025
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Conflict & Choice

*The home is a home until it isn’t,*

Etched in the brain like malignant cankers,

This suffering in soul doesn’t yield or wither,

The pain felt too deep to be physical or whole.

Just sore, and grey, and scarring its soldiers,

This war once waged to reclaim a hearth,

This home now a nightmare,

This home lost its laugh.

Should one say when it happened,

Or so suspects when it changed,

That night, on that weekend, when the world seemed unruly,

Bathing in bliss and fighting fear’s folds.

Returned to see carnage,

A bloodbath,

Brought tears.

*The home is a home if you fight off invaders,*

Yet the stench lingers clean, glued to walls and dulled papers,

This home now a nightmare,

This home now a farce,

This home now unsafe,

Fools the fool, this façade.
