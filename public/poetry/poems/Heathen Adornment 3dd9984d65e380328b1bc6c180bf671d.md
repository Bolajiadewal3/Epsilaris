# Heathen Adornment

Date: December 4, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Nature-focused
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

Starlight, spectres of nebulous dust,
Crammed in the iris of one small heathen,
Lord leaves me wonders,
Holy in husk.

Yet this one bewitched me,
Leaves my eyes bleeding.

From gazing for hours,
Through her dim fires,
The window is open,
The soul now in flux.

This one rejects reason,
She works on the Sun-
Day, when my feelings laid bare to Him: judged.
