# Dormant

Date: June 7, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Transformation & Growth

In the footsteps of giants,
I lose track of the dew and desires.

For I be mere flesh chasing half-spawn of angels,
Monsters or mages, flesh lost in the fire.

The pure burns bright like the heart of the phoenix,
Reborn as a hunter, paving his way.

In the footsteps of giants, my crudeness is tamed,
Blood dormant and waiting to meet Angel ways.
