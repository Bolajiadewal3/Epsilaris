# Serene

Date: May 17, 2025
Movement: Romanticism
Sub-Theme One: Romantic
Theme: Love & Desire

Serene like the pit of an angel,
Holy is the husk,
Distracted by visions of vagrants,
Your face saintly, perfect, loved.

Serene like the scent of a halo,
Want to know you like we had love,
In a past life our souls were unnerving,
Two minds perfect, connected, lust.
