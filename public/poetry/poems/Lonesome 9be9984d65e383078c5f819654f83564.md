# Lonesome

Date: July 30, 2025
Movement: Modernism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Sub-Theme Two: Free Verse
Theme: Identity & Isolation

Again, the mire writes about the lonesome,
The miser does deem their own soul: ajar,
The maker or mystic: depends on your eyes,
A maker of mischief or mystic with fires.

Same fires that bleed onto oil-ridden frames,
Canvases coated with angelic phrase,
Describing the words as if gauche for Goya,
Derelict, dark mind of mine preaches in pyres.
