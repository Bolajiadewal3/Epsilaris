# Qualia

Date: June 18, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

Is my red, your red?

Or my minute, yours?

Is that flutter from my flame,

Mirrored by your core?

For that red I see is dangerous,

Violent yet serene.

Your red by your admission,

Is life, perfect, clean.

Could count a thousand more,

Mere numbers void of meaning,

Yet eyes of yours see digits,

In shapes, words and feelings.

Is my minute yours?

This time we share, how so?

My world does seem so fleeting,

While yours, too slow and dull.
