# Luna

Date: September 10, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Cosmic
Theme: Nature & Cosmos

The dim specks of childish stars paint harsher hues on this canvas,
This critical crevice of skin and bone, assaulted by rays of starlight.

This phase, anew, aghast with violence,
Attacking the souls of the beasts born crude,
Once babes now blistered by hairs or furs,
These darlings were something, now: “some things” askew.

A few days more then repentance and bleeding,
A few days more and the smiles disappear,
A few days more and the waning is weaving,
Itself cross the face of the moon evermore.

A week left of wondrous delight at the teething,
The sounds of the “some things” would scare any moor,
The forest is creeping, alive with the motion,
Of beasts born anew, the moon lights their core.
