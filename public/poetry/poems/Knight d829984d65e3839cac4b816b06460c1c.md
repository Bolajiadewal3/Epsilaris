# Knight

Date: November 7, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Conflict & Choice

In the steps of a giants. One craves,
A new token of praise, and a taste,
For some power with, sainthood in tow.

Kings killed,
Took gold,
Stole, souls.

Crept forward slaying, the helpless saved,
By his foe: knight of the scared, made haste,
Crushed the foul, crueler men; slew the slow.
