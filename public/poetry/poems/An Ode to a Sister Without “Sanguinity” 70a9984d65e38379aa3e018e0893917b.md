# An Ode to a Sister Without “Sanguinity”

Date: March 27, 2025
Movement: Symbolism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Sub-Theme Two: Rhymed & Metrical
Theme: Love & Desire

While my faith has been tested, withered and wailed,
I still pray for your heart, liver and tail,
Of no blood but our kinship is holy and frail,
Cells from mine do differ, for that I have failed.

For how may I claim: divinity or fame,
Yet have no new power to shape your red frames,
With tears in the eyes and hands clenched to fire,
I whisper to gods who may bless you entire.

Chain my soul,
bind my Bowels,
stole a flame,
killed her hounds,
Promethean pyre of ruinous delight,
Still pray to lords to stem the sound.

With tears in eyes my heart doth speak,
Don’t take my sister, do take me,
Sisyphus boulder: my burden to bear
Let her see skies, let me see stairs.

Burden of proof that my life did prove fixed,
Let blood in her flow like this river, this Styx.
