# Blame

Date: November 13, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Grief & Loss

Blame me,
Blame us,
Until their eyes reach mine.

Blame them,
Blame lust,
When their actions twist a knife,
Into my mind and wrench it out,
Embrassed for all time,
Erased from sight, sense and sign,
This child no longer mine.
