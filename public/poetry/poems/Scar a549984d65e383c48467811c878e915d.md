# Scar

Date: September 9, 2025
Movement: Confessional Poetry
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Transformation & Growth

There’s a beauty in blisters,
A pleasure in pus.

A moment of mischief that lead to dark dealings,
Birthed paradise of seconds: how fleeting the motion.

Joint pain-pleasure pistols did fix self-willed woes:

There’s a beauty in breaks,
Either brain or the bone:
If all lived with no loss,
None would cherish their folk.

There’s a beauty in bile,
One’s own struggle feels whole,
Persevere through the pain:
Opens worlds for true folk.
