# Dream

Date: November 7, 2025
Movement: Surrealism
Sub-Theme One: Mythic & Occult
Theme: Conflict & Choice

For the battle has started, swords clash,
Armour rattles, attacked by rude scores,
Of the splatters, the blood, seeps in pores.

Swords thrusts,
Deep down,
Through flesh.

Cracks and the screams with their crazed unseen,
Crests of esteemed now are bloodied, reamed,
Crude is the dream where in death, is them.
