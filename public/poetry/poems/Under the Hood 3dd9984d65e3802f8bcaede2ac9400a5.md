# Under the Hood

Date: September 16, 2025
Movement: Surrealism
Sub-Theme One: Introspective
Theme: Identity & Isolation

Sometimes,
The brain bleeds in modes unseen,
Caving to crooked and crazed afflictions.

Sometimes,
The mind melts in the mire,
Collapsed and clawing its way through the world.

Sometimes,
The sky erupts with a roar,
Rapturous applause only heard by one.

Sometimes,
The blood soars to the surface,
Painting the world with noise and notes.

The innards of savants and savage alike,
Removed from the conscious thought of their workings,
That inner voice speaks on its own: discerning,
Itself as a victim, damaged in dose.
